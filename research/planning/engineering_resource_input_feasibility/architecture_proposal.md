# Engineering resource input interface feasibility proposal

**Independent review draft · 26 November 2026**

PMI can support a future interface for capturing engineering and resource inputs as versioned drafts. The existing 22-input catalog and the B6 crosswalk provide a usable field vocabulary. Scheduling these inputs for an additional IFC model depends on a non-fixture worker, a qualified model and supported work scope, and verified engineering decisions. The current R0 viewer and its registered replay remain a frozen demonstration.

## Handover findings

- **B6 crosswalk and input workbook.** Agent 2 matched the 22 input IDs and the 24 contract groups to the repository. The binding template marks the input rows `missing`. B6 provides the structure for project settings, calendars, zones, crews, equipment, concrete and holds. The hypothetical calendar, crews and holiday override belong to fixture data. Real capacities and project settings require their own evidence.
- **B4 formwork.** Face rules, junction deductions and scope determine contact area for estimating. Physical panel and prop capacities, per-activity reservation demand, release conditions and reuse delay are separate declarations. BCCA equipment cost shares and generic IFC surface areas cannot supply those quantities. B4's worked examples are hypothetical.
- **B5 reinforcement.** The BOQ template can inform evidence submission by model element or approved group allocation. A designer bar schedule, rebar model or documented design mass must establish kg. Check steel grade, bars versus mesh, and double counting. Catalogue rates are reference inputs for estimation.
- **B8 review.** The original 75 rows are pending. Use the repository register and its 13 AP-X addendum rows as decision references. Map overlapping B8 proposals to existing decisions and send unmatched questions, such as the curing/striking combination, to qualified review. The 13 rows cover calendars, fixed crew modes, reusable formwork/props and release, inspection, strength-release evidence, support sequencing, split work and concrete supply. All 88 decisions remain pending, AP-TOL has no approved tolerance, and real-project eligibility is false.

## Proposed workflow

1. **Draft capture.** Start a versioned project/model record. Key entries by the existing `input_id_or_path` and `record_key`, scoped to project, model revision and element, zone, activity or resource as applicable. For IFC-bound entries, retain the source file SHA-256 and resolve full GlobalIds within that revision. Record any grouping explicitly.
2. **Validate.** Apply field types, enums, units and applicability rules at the service boundary, followed by cross-field and model checks. Preserve entered units alongside normalized units and deterministic conversions. Populate the B6 binding fields for destination, value, unit, input state, evidence reference and applicable approval IDs. Represent missing values as null, and record non-applicability with a reason. Require an explicit supported declaration and evidence for zero capacity when it means a resource is unused. An explicit false is a decision; omission is unknown.
3. **Review.** Keep the value's provenance, validation result and engineering authorization as distinct records. Require document revision, author, date and scope for supplied evidence, and tool, units, model revision and method for measurement evidence. Store reviewer identity, authority, signed decision reference, date and conditions against the exact input revision and scope. Material edits or model revisions should require a fresh applicability check. A project manager can supply or transcribe evidence without approving it.
4. **Project to downstream inputs.** A future server-side adapter would map applicable eligible records to Stage 5.2 classification/quantity inputs, Stage 5.3 estimates and the normalized scheduling contract. Only form eligible activities and resource constraints for the supported scope. Preserve blocked, conditional and subset outcomes explicitly. Supply registry authority from the host service. The current normalized runtime rejects request-supplied `engineer_approved` and therefore cannot use a frontend approval toggle. A future independently reviewed authorization path is needed for real-project use.
5. **Read-only result.** A future result viewer would consume a provenance-linked schedule generated from a frozen input revision. CPM and CP-SAT stay downstream of the input service. The accepted Stage 5.4D viewer remains R0-only and the exporter remains restricted to registered R0 content.

`missing` blocks applicable downstream use. `hypothetical_fixture` stays in a fixture namespace and is visibly marked as a demonstration. `user_provided_project` requires source attribution. `independently_measured` requires traceable supporting evidence and is distinct from engineering approval. Store genuine project entries in their own draft namespace with no fixture defaults or promotion path. Typical scenario values and the unverified 1 March 2027 holiday claim cannot initialize real-project records.

## Candidate frontend fields

The [22-row field mapping](field_mapping.csv) specifies the user label, data type/unit, destination, validation, missing handling, provenance, required review, editable role and downstream consumer. Its consumer column distinguishes supported R0 analogues from future routes.

Operational sections are the clearest candidates for PM or contractor draft editing. They include crew counts and hours (UI14), calendar and administrative fields (UI15), zone proposals (UI16), and supplier and equipment availability (UI19). PMs could attach or transcribe specifications and measured evidence for the remaining inputs. Engineering selections should be editable only in the appropriate review workflow, including classification, quantity bases, design selections, lift methods, curing, inspection and release. Identity resolution, eligibility, derived quantities, authenticated evidence state and solver outputs are read-only projections. Six areas can use the B6 material as an initial design reference without entering values into scheduling: calendars, crews, capacities, formwork plans, reinforcement evidence and inspection/strength gates.

## Dependencies for additional IFC models

| Dependency | Evidence or completion condition |

| I04 non-fixture scheduling worker | Replace the fixture dispatch for new-model runs with a worker that consumes the actual IFC-derived engineering handover, normalizes stable IDs, calendars, activities, dependencies, resources and events, and invokes the existing CPM/CP-SAT components. Verify it independently on non-R0 inputs. The current R0 worker builds from `frozen_r0`, and Stage 5.3 measurement ingestion also contains fixture-specific handling. |
| IFC model and scope qualification | Validate source revision, IFC schema, units, placements, stable identities, and quantity and material coverage. Resolve candidate classifications and overlap/measurement bases. Qualify catalogue and construction methods for the target scope. Earthworks, masonry and precast rows in this mapping retain upstream destinations pending specific support. |
| Quantity evidence and decisions | AP-TOL and relevant measurement decisions remain pending. QV09 needs full edge remeasurement, geometry and identity evidence. QV18 needs correct element re-identification and measurement. QV23 and QV30 need full identity evidence, and QV23 also needs the AP-DC06 depth basis. Other intake evidence gaps remain and the R0 Tier A measurement exercise is unstarted. IFC-imported Revit dimensions are a reference cross-check. |
| Real project data and approvals | Obtain actual design quantities, work methods, crews, resource availability, project location/calendar/timezone, equipment and supply. Obtain authorization for the applicable register and addendum decisions, including inspection and strength events and support/asset release when relevant. Validate evidence and scope before any eligibility decision. |
| Resource contract coverage | Carry calendar exceptions and local-date capacity through R3-F07, physical reservations and releases through R1/R2, and the accepted split-pour supply reporting rules. Validate distinctions among planned placed volume, restart allowance and actual observations. Controls shown under related UI IDs do not expand the 22-ID catalog. |
| Model export and viewer acceptance | Generalize the controlled IFC4D trust boundary only after new-model qualification. Define how derived schedules and model artifacts are authenticated, then reverify round-trip geometry, stable identities, task links, dependencies and restrictions. Existing R0-only fingerprint and shape checks continue to govern the current viewer. |
